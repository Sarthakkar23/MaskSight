# MaskSight demo

Label-free defect segmentation. Upload a product image, pick its type, and get an anomaly heatmap
plus a binary defect mask. Models were fit on normal MVTec AD images only. Source and benchmark:
https://github.com/Sarthakkar23/MaskSight
